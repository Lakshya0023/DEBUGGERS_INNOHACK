Core Features

Everything You Need for Land Management

A complete ecosystem for transparent, accountable, and citizen-friendly land administration.

🗺️

Interactive Map Explorer

Click any point on the map to instantly fetch land parcel details — owner, area, survey number, price history, and encumbrance status.

📋

Complete Ownership History

View the full chain of ownership — every transfer, deed number, consideration amount, and transfer type going back decades.

📊

Price Trend Analytics

Track market value and government-assessed value trends over 5+ years with beautiful interactive charts.

📝

Grievance Redressal

File complaints about disputes, encroachments, corruption, or document issues. Track your ticket in real-time with a unique ID.

🔐

Secure Authentication

JWT-based secure login for both citizens and administrators. Role-based access ensures data privacy and audit trails.

⚡

Mutation Management

Submit ownership transfer requests digitally. Admins approve, creating a full audit trail with automatic history updates.
