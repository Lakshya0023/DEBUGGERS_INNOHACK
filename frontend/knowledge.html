Complete Land Records Guide

All 8 record types, DILRMP programme, 5-step online process, state-wise info

8 Key Record Types Across India

1. Khasra — Plot Level Record (UP, MP, RJ)

Records survey number, area, soil type, crop, owner/cultivator. Each plot's unique "land ID" in the revenue system.

2. Khatauni — Land Holdings Register (UP)

Lists all plots owned by one person in a village. Summary of total holdings. Updated every 5 years by Patwari.

3. 7/12 Satbara Utara — Maharashtra

Form 7 (ownership) + Form 12 (crop). Most widely referenced document. Essential for property transactions, bank loans, government schemes.

4. RTC / Pahani — Karnataka (Bhoomi)

Record of Rights, Tenancy and Crops. Shows owner, tenancy, land type, area, crop details. Maintained by Village Accountant.

5. Patta / Chitta — Tamil Nadu

Patta = primary ownership document. Chitta = land classification (wet/dry/house-site). Together they serve as the RoR for Tamil Nadu.

6. Adangal / 1B — AP / Telangana

Crop cultivation, land area, irrigation source, farmer name. Linked with Pattadar Passbook and Bhudhaar. New Bhu Bharathi Act 2025.

7. Jamabandi / Fard — Rajasthan, Haryana, Punjab

Khewat (ownership share) + Khasra (plot) + Khatauni (account). Updated every 4 years by Patwari. Apna Khata portal.

8. Khata Certificate — Municipal Record

Urban equivalent. Proves property is in BBMP/municipal records. Required for property tax, building plan approval, bank loans.

DILRMP — Digital India Land Records Programme

The DILRMP was launched in 2016 under 100% central funding (Rs.875 crore). As of December 2023, computerisation covers 95.09% of villages. Sub-Registrar integration exceeds 93%.
