🔒 Cyber Security Guidelines
UP Cyber Security Guidelines — Bhulekh Portal
⚠️ Please read these guidelines carefully before using the Land Services Portal to protect your data.
🔐
Never share your OTP or password with anyone — not even revenue department officials. Government portals will never ask for your password.
🌐
Always verify the URL before entering Aadhaar or personal details. Ensure the site uses HTTPS and the domain is official.
📱
Do not use public Wi-Fi or shared computers to access land records linked to Aadhaar. Use private networks only.
📩
Beware of phishing emails claiming to be from Bhulekh/BhoomiSeva. Official communications will only come from .gov.in domains.
💳
Certified copy payment should only be done through official state government payment gateways. Avoid third-party payment links.
🔍
Regularly verify your land records online to detect any unauthorized mutations or transfers on your property.
📞
Report suspected fraud to the Cyber Crime Cell: 1930 (National Helpline) or your state's revenue department helpline immediately.
🏛
No government official should be demanding cash or digital payments for land record services. Report bribery to the Anti-Corruption Bureau.
🔄
Keep browser updated. Use only official apps from state government websites. Do not use unofficial third-party apps claiming to show land records.
📋
Download records only from authenticated sessions. QR-code signed documents are the only legally valid downloadable copies.
